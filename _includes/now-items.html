{%- comment -%} Now items for /now and the homepage. include.max (optional) caps how many show. An item with `until: YYYY-MM-DD` is dropped at build time once it is more than a day past (build clock is UTC), and hidden in the browser from the day after `until` (visitor's local date), so a finished event leaves /now and the homepage on time even if nobody pushes. The next visible item moves up to keep the homepage at max. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign now_cutoff = site.time | date: "%s" | minus: 86400 | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- assign now_max = include.max | default: 999 -%} {%- assign now_shown = 0 -%}
