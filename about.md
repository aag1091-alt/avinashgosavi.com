# About Avinash Gosavi

Hi, I'm Avinash Gosavi! I live in the San Francisco Bay Area and work as a Staff Software Engineer at Cisco Meraki. My journey in software development started in 2011 with Ruby, a language that has remained my favorite ever since. In 2014, I moved to the U.S. to pursue my Master's in Computer Science at Old Dominion University—Go Monarchs! I've called the Bay Area home since 2019.

Outside of work, I'm an avid Arsenal fan and love exploring coffee and food spots in the area. Lately, I've also developed an interest in Lego and Board games.

At Cisco Meraki I've spent five years bringing Cisco Catalyst switches, wireless and routers under Meraki cloud management, and building the APIs that pull it all together. Off hours I build privacy-first AI tools — encrypted memory for agents, local-first voice apps — and read papers on how memory works, in brains and in machines.

This site is where I share that work: [projects](/projects/), [writing](/blog/), the [papers and talks](/research/) that shaped my thinking, and the [conferences](/events/) I keep showing up to. There's something about coding that never gets boring for me.

**Ways to work together:** I'm happy to pair, review a design, judge a hackathon, or talk about agent memory, API design at scale, or Rails architecture. Reach out — you can find me at most places under **@aag1091**.
